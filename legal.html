Who we are

The Ecosystem Scouting program is run by the Advanced Data Architecture practice of [Accenture legal entity, e.g. Accenture S.p.A.], [registered address], which acts as data controller for the personal data collected through this site ("we", "us").

What we collect

When you submit your company through the form we collect:

  • Contact details of the person submitting: name, role, work e-mail address and, if provided, phone number and LinkedIn profile.
  • Information about your company and product: name, website, country, size, funding stage, product capabilities, deployment and licensing model, customers and references, certifications, partnership preferences, and any free-text notes you add.
  • Technical information about the submission: a reference number, date and time, the version of the form, and the browser language.

Most of this information concerns your company, not you as an individual. The personal data is limited to the contact details and to names that may appear in free-text fields.

Why we process it

  • To evaluate whether your product fits the programs our practice delivers and to contact you about it. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in identifying technology partners, and your consent given when you send the form.
  • To keep a record of vendors we have assessed, so we can come back to you when a relevant program appears. Legal basis: legitimate interest.
  • If you opt in, to inform you about future scouting programs and events of the practice. Legal basis: consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Who receives it

  • Members of the Advanced Data Architecture practice and, where relevant, other Accenture teams involved in evaluating or deploying your technology.
  • Service providers that operate the submission form on our behalf: [form provider, e.g. Web3Forms / Formspree / Microsoft], acting as processors under contract.
  • We do not sell your data and do not share it with other vendors.

How long we keep it

Submissions are kept for [24 months] from the date of submission, so we can match your product with programs that start later. You can ask us to delete your submission at any time.

Data processing

This page describes, in plain terms, how a submission travels:

  • The form runs in your browser. Your progress is saved only on your device until you press "Send submission".
  • When you send, the answers are transmitted over an encrypted connection to [form provider], which delivers them to the scouting team's mailbox and stores a copy as part of its service. [If the e-mail option is used: the answers are placed in an e-mail that you send from your own e-mail client.]
  • Before sending, the form computes a preliminary classification (product area, maturity, readiness, fit) from your answers. This is a triage aid for the scouting team and is not a decision about your company.
  • The scouting team stores accepted submissions in an internal tracker accessible to the practice.
